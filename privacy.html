Voidhive

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version

  • Voidhive has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects device data, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time (see Your choices).

Who we are

Voidhive is a mobile game developed and published by Gaming Habits ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at gaminghabits1998@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps the following on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Cores balance, permanent upgrades, unlocked gear and skins, and your loadout
  • Best times, best level and arena progress
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration and graphics quality
  • Counters used to pace how often ads appear

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Voidhive is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. Some ads are optional rewarded videos (for example to revive or to double your cores). Others are full-screen ads shown between runs.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google uses this information to deliver, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve its services. We only receive aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown. We do not receive information that identifies you.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy → Manage in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Optional ads: rewarded video ads are only shown when you tap a button to watch one.

Sale and sharing of personal information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Google's ad software may use device identifiers for personalized advertising, which some US state privacy laws consider "sharing". You can opt out of this by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization in your phone's settings, as described above.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load and show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ad software, as described above.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback when you are hit (you can turn this off in Settings).

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep your personal data on our own servers. Your game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's own policies, and you can manage it through your Google account and phone settings.

Security

Your game data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Google states that data collected by its ad software is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that handle data differently, such as online accounts, cloud saves or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and change the effective date above.
