Delete your data

Voidhive: Swarm Survivor, by Gaming Habits

The short version

  • Voidhive has no accounts of its own, and we (Gaming Habits) do not keep your data on our servers.
  • Your progress is stored on your phone. If you use cloud save, a copy is also stored by Google in Google Play Games.
  • You can delete both yourself at any time with the steps below. If you need help, email gaminghabits1998@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

1. Delete the game data on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings and tap Apps.
  2. Tap Voidhive, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear data (or Clear storage). Uninstalling the game also deletes this data.

What is deleted: cores and Void Gems balances, upgrades, unlocked gear, skins and trails, loadout, arena progress, best times and statistics, mission progress and login streak, settings, and ad counters. It is deleted from the phone right away.

2. Delete your cloud save (Google Play Games)

Only needed if you signed in under Settings → Cloud save in the game.

  1. Open the Google Play Games app, or the Play Games section of the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Voidhive and tap Delete.

What is deleted: the Voidhive saved game stored in your Google account, and Voidhive's link to your Play Games profile (player name and player ID). Your Play Games profile for other games is not affected. Google stores this data and deletes it under Google's Privacy Policy.

If you do not delete your phone's game data too, the game will upload it again the next time you sign in.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID. To delete or reset it, open your phone's Settings and go to Privacy → Ads (on some phones Google → Ads), then tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. Google keeps and deletes ad data under its own policies.

What is kept

  • Purchases: Google Play keeps your purchase history and receipts in your Google account, as required for payments and refunds. One-time purchases (Remove Ads and packs) are restored if you reinstall. Unspent Void Gems are part of the game data and are deleted with it.
  • We (Gaming Habits) keep no data about you, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.
